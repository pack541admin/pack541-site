Our Pack

Pack 541 serves families from PCE, Ladera, Hicks Canyon, TMA, Orchard Hills, and Tustin Ranch Elementary schools. We believe scouting builds confidence, teamwork, and lifelong memories — all while having a great time outdoors.

Our leaders are all volunteers — parents just like you — committed to making each Scout year unforgettable. No experience required. We'll show you the ropes.

We are all volunteers with the simple goal of having our Scouts learn and experience new things.
— Pack 541 Leadership
