The Parent's Role

Every Scout has an adult partner — called an Akela — who attends meetings and helps them complete rank requirements. This can be a parent, grandparent, or other trusted adult over 18.

Pack 541 is entirely volunteer-run, so every family is expected to help in one way or another — whether that's a committee role, running an event, or just showing up and pitching in. See Volunteering and Adult Roles for ways to get involved.
